What you can build

Process flows with up to three levels of nested decisions. Each step carries an actor chip (USER / BACKEND / MANAGER or any custom role you declare), an optional description, and optional audit-event pills. Branches carry color through their entire path — outcome pills, terminal-state cards, and the arrows that lead into them.
